# Code of conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and do not harass anyone. Report unacceptable behavior
to team@fonoster.com. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that break
these rules, and may ban those responsible.
